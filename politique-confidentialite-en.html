Who is responsible for your data?

FDA, a French single-member limited liability company (SARL) with a share capital of €10,000, registered with the Tours trade register under number 889 822 300, with its registered office at La Bonde, 37230 Fondettes, France, publisher of the game Door Knight. Contact: fda.doorknight@gmail.com.

We have not appointed a data protection officer; all requests are handled at this address.

The doorknight.games website

  • No cookies, no audience measurement, no ads. The website sets no tracker that requires consent, which is why no cookie banner is shown.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any host, GitHub receives your IP address and the technical details of your visit (browser, page requested) to display the site and protect it against abuse. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Fonts, images and videos are hosted on the website itself: no data is sent to Google Fonts or any other third-party service during your visit.
  • Your browser's memory. The website keeps two settings in your browser: the language you picked (localStorage, until you clear the site's data) and, for the length of the visit (sessionStorage), whether you turned the music off or on. They never leave your device.
  • External links. The buttons to TestFlight, the App Store and Discord take you to those services, which apply their own privacy policies.

The Door Knight game

What the game saves on your device

Your progress (doors cleared, level, gold, upgrades, hero look, quests, Pass, settings) is saved only on your device. The game has no player account with an email or real name and does not send us this information. Uninstalling the game deletes this save.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address, technical information and data about ad display, to serve and measure ads and, if you agree, to personalize them. In Europe, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form appears at first launch; you can change your mind at any time with the "Privacy and ads" link on the home screen. On iPhone, ad tracking only happens if you allow it in Apple's prompt. How Google ads work.

In-app purchases (App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play: we never have access to your payment details. To check purchases and allow them to be restored, the RevenueCat service receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app and the history of purchases made in the game. RevenueCat policy. The Royal Club is a monthly subscription renewed automatically until you cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Statistics (Firebase Analytics)

If you agree (the question is asked in the game in Europe; elsewhere it is on by default and can be turned off from the home screen), the game sends usage statistics to Google Firebase: doors played, screens shown, purchases, device model and approximate country. They are used only to improve the game and are not used for advertising.

Nickname, leaderboard and arena

For the leaderboard, the game creates an anonymous Firebase account (no email or real name) and stores on Google Firebase servers: your nickname and its number, your level, your best door, your weekly points, your hero's look and your Royal Club status. This information is visible to other players. During an arena fight, the game exchanges with your opponent your nickname, level, class, hero look, rating and fight state; this fight data is deleted at the end of the match. Nickname reports are kept for moderation.

Notifications

If you agree, the game schedules local reminders (camp treasure, daily gift). They are created by the app itself, with no server and no identifier sent, and can be turned off in your phone's settings.

Discord community, TestFlight beta and contact

  • Discord. On the Door Knight server, your messages are processed by Discord under its own policy. Posts in the #bugs, #idées, #gameplay, #classes and #décors forums are automatically copied, with your Discord name, the date and attachments, into private tracking tickets on GitHub, to fix bugs and study ideas.
  • TestFlight beta. If you join the beta, Apple sends us information about your testing: device and iOS version, installs, sessions, crash reports and feedback you send from TestFlight. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Emails. If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

Who receives this data?

Only FDA and its technical processors: GitHub (website and ticket hosting), Google (AdMob, Firebase, Gmail for emails), RevenueCat (purchases), Apple (App Store, TestFlight) and Discord (community). We sell no data.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or otherwise by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Minors

Door Knight is intended for players aged 13 and over. In France, if you are under 15, consent to personalized ads and statistics must be given together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if this happens, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing or port it, object to processing based on legitimate interest, withdraw your consent at any time, and set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

Write to fda.doorknight@gmail.com with your nickname (and its number) or your Discord name. We reply within one month. As the game does not create a named account, ad data can also be managed directly with Google, and the advertising identifier can be reset in your phone's settings.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority): cnil.fr/en/complaints, or with the authority of the country where you live.

Changes

This policy may change with the game and the website. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
